House of XII / updated 2026-11-12

Privacy

This policy covers the XII app for iPhone (House of XII), run by XII Studios, doing business as XII Majiq Studios.

On your phone

Your card, page, desk (releases, split sheets, documents) and the list of requests you sent are stored on this phone.

In your iCloud

Releases and documents are saved in your own iCloud by Apple so they sync and can be shared. Only the people you invite can see them. XII cannot read your private iCloud data.

Sent to XII

Applications and requests are sent by email to xiistudios@xiimajiqstudios.com, with what you typed in them. XII uses them to review your seat and answer your request.

Sign in with Apple and location

If you sign in with Apple, the name and email you choose to share stay on this phone and fill in your applications and requests; XII sees them only in what you send. Your location is used only on this phone to find studios near you; it is not stored or sent to XII.

What XII does not do

No ads, no tracking across apps, no selling or renting your information.

Your choices

Delete anything on your desk in the app. To delete everything at once, open the House tab and tap Delete my account and data: it removes your card, page, desk and the releases and documents you own from this phone and your iCloud, and signs you out.

To remove your application, requests or member record from the house inbox, email xiistudios@xiimajiqstudios.com. XII removes them within 30 days unless the law requires keeping them.

Children

The house is for people 18 and older. XII does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

XII may update this summary; the date at the top changes when it does. Questions about privacy go to xiistudios@xiimajiqstudios.com. Full policy: https://jaydeecoder.github.io/house-of-xii-legal/privacy
